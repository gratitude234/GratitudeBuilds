## The opportunity

Campus commerce already exists, but it is fragmented across conversations, informal posts, and disconnected vendors. JabuMarket creates one local place to discover what is available and reach the right person quickly.

<DecisionCallout label="Product principle">
  Start with the campus as it really works. Make local trust, availability, and direct communication part of the product.
</DecisionCallout>

## One market, several everyday needs

The product brings general listings, food, services, delivery, and messaging into one system. The information architecture makes those behaviours feel connected while keeping each path easy to understand.

<ProductVisual variant="market" expanded caption="The marketplace system before campus launch." />

## Designing for fast decisions

Marketplace interfaces live or die on clarity. Categories, prices, vendor status, and contact paths are surfaced early so a student can move from browsing to action without unnecessary steps.

<FlowDiagram items="Find a need|Compare locally|Open the listing|Talk or order" />

## Built from the foundation up

I own JabuMarket and built its product strategy, interface, application behaviour, and supporting systems from scratch. The current focus is launch readiness rather than inflated traction claims.

## What comes next

JabuMarket has not yet been introduced to students. The next milestone is a focused campus launch, followed by observation of search, listing, vendor, and order behaviour before expanding the product.
